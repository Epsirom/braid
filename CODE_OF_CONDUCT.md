# Code of conduct

Participants should be able to ask questions, disagree, and contribute without
harassment. Be respectful, assume good faith, focus criticism on the work, and
accept feedback. Welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience,
or ability. English and Chinese contributions are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing,
and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information without permission.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where
participants represent Braid. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content,
warn participants, limit interaction, or ban repeated or serious violations.
Actions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns to [Epsirom](https://github.com/Epsirom) using a contact method on
the GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. If no private contact
is available, or a report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's Report abuse
feature. Reports are handled discreetly, with information shared only as needed
to address the incident. This is a volunteer project; response times are not
guaranteed.
